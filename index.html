How to Play

1

Choose your bet and difficulty. Your available range is loading….

2

Press Play, then Go to drop one container at a time. Every safe block raises your payout multiplier.

3

Cash out whenever you like after one safe block—or keep stacking for a higher multiplier.

Difficulty & blocks

Easy24 blocks
Medium22 blocks
Hard20 blocks

Multiplier & payout

  • The current multiplier appears on the Cash Out button after each safe block.
  • Harder difficulties offer fewer blocks and a steeper risk/reward progression.
  • Your displayed cash-out value is an estimate; the backend-confirmed winning amount is final.
  • Completing the final block automatically wins and pays out—no Cash Out is required.
If a block crashes, the round ends and the bet is lost. Only bet what you are comfortable losing.
